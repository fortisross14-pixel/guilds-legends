# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players on phones, tablets, and desktop who want a long-running fantasy guild-management RPG. They return in short sessions to make meaningful preparations, play tactical battles, and follow the institutional history of a guild across generations.

## Product Purpose

Guilds of Legend lets a player found, manage, and remember a fantasy guild: recruit heroes, plan expeditions, enter tournaments, build a headquarters, and leave a durable record in a deterministic world.

## Positioning

It combines personal JRPG-style hero attachment with a persistent guild simulation: individual careers, rival institutions, rankings, and world history endure for centuries from a reproducible seed.

## Operating Context

The primary loop is discover, prepare, commit, experience, celebrate or regret, progress, and remember. Play alternates between world travel, town destinations, party preparation, battles or mission games, and archival review. The primary interaction is touch; tablet landscape and desktop support richer spatial views.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React 18 and Vite 5 application. The deterministic game engine and its save/IndexedDB format remain product truth; the UI is a thin layer over engine actions. Existing pixel-art portraits, sprites, enemies, effects, and scenes in `public/art/` must be preserved and framed. Preserve all gameplay access, keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, GitHub Pages asset loading, and mobile-safe layouts. No unnecessary dependencies.

## Brand Commitments

Guilds of Legend is a premium, tactile fantasy RPG experience—not a SaaS dashboard or a dusty medieval website. Its identity combines expressive pixel art, a living fantasy world, clear console-game interaction, and emotional investment in heroes and their history. Class drives hero artwork, rarity drives frames, and primal alignment drives environmental treatment; rarity and primal must remain distinct systems.

## Evidence on Hand

Game content, deterministic game systems, and visual assets live in this repository. The creative direction supplied by the user is authoritative; no external brand claims or visual references are to be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the next meaningful decision unmistakable without reducing the world to a checklist.
- Treat heroes, victories, losses, and records as memories rather than rows of data.
- Make ritual actions—launching, recruiting, entering, winning—feel consequential.
- Let tactile clarity lead on small screens; use added space to deepen immersion rather than densify administration.
- Keep the living world and the guild’s changing place in it legible at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls are usable by keyboard and assistive technology, touch targets are comfortable, contrast is strong, focus is visible, and reduced-motion preference is respected.
